      Starter pantry

      Adds a deduped set of your usual groceries with per-serving nutrition. Every item is unverified — the first time you log one, check it against the real label, then it's saved verified. Safe to tap again; it skips foods you already have.

      Cross-references each estimate against real H-E-B / Kirkland SKUs in the USDA branded database and lets you pick the exact one — adopting its label numbers and marking it verified. Needs your own USDA key (below) for the full run.

      Food search

      Name search uses the USDA FoodData Central database. The shared demo key works for light use but is rate-limited — get your own free key in a minute at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup and paste it above. Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts and need no key.

      Data

      Everything is stored on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere except the food-search lookups you trigger.
